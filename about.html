About Squeaky Clean Housekeeping Services

A locally owned cleaning company focused on dependable service, professionalism and customer care.

A Cleaning Company Built on Trust

Squeaky Clean Housekeeping Services was founded with a simple goal: to provide dependable cleaning services while treating every client with professionalism and care.

We serve both residential and commercial clients throughout Atlanta and surrounding Metro Atlanta communities.

Our services range from routine housekeeping and deep cleaning to commercial janitorial programs, medical office cleaning, restaurant cleaning, warehouse cleaning and other property-specific cleaning needs.

We focus on building long-term client relationships through reliable service and consistent attention to detail.
